Autonomous Driving Challenge with AWS DeepRacer

August 2024 - December 2024

Built a 1/18-scale autonomous vehicle using ROS 2 Humble on Raspberry Pi 4, navigating via RGB camera-based blue line following and LiDAR odometry.
